Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Math Masters Formula Encyclopedia ("the App") is developed by an independent developer. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or tracking frameworks
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers

2. AI Features

The App uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models for AI-powered Q&A. All AI processing happens entirely on your device. No queries or responses are sent to any server. This feature requires iPhone 15 Pro or later with iOS 18.1+.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs these transactions.

4. Local Data Storage

The App stores your preferences, favorites, and learning progress locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync (future feature), this data is stored in your private iCloud container, accessible only to you.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. All content and functionality is self-contained.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
